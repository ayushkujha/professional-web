Student Enrollment

Chapter Membership & Onboarding (AY 2026–2027)

Benefits of Chapter Membership

Undergraduate and postgraduate students of Vidya Jyothi Institute of Technology may enroll in the VJIT ACM Student Chapter to access global publications and campus training tracks.

  • Access to the ACM Digital Library, Communications of the ACM, and XRDS student journal.
  • Weekly peer-mentored sessions across our three Special Interest Groups (AI/ML, Competitive Programming, Full-Stack Systems).
  • Priority registration for CodeStorm, LAUNCHPAD, and ACM India Eminent Speaker lectures.
  • Eligibility to serve on the Student Executive Committee and organize departmental symposia.

Verification Procedure

1. Complete the onboarding form using your 10-character JNTUH/VJIT Roll Number (e.g., 25911A6601) and official @vjit.ac.in email.

2. Quote your generated provisional reference code to the Chapter Secretary or Treasurer at the Department of CSE (AI & ML).
